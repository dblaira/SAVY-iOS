## Why the usual YouTube systems kill the motivation

- Adam’s Connection: “I am a polymath: therefore specialization is a kill switch.  Volume is the only way to sustainable interest.  Volume is beautiful because volume is where patterns can be seen.”

- Adam: “One of my greatest strengths is pattern recognition. When things are too organized I check out, or get bored and struggle to focus.”

- Adam’s words, rule 5: “What pulls instead of pushes”

- A passage Adam pasted and asked an agent to follow: “You don’t build the filing cabinet first. You let the pile accumulate, then notice what clusters together.”

The usual advice says to pick a niche, plan content pillars and fill a calendar first. That is the filing cabinet built before the pile, so it pushes. A shining example that connects to two unrelated ones pulls.

**My dot:** Three of Adam’s Connections describe one pattern.

- “I think more like venture capital than private equity.”

- “I think of volume as this wonderful outcome. It’s the only thing I can sustain. 30 and get up to 300. I want 300.”

- “I am a polymath: therefore specialization is a kill switch.”

Venture capital makes many bets because a few winners pay for everything. YouTube works the same way: a few videos carry most of a channel’s views. So volume comes first, and the viewers’ numbers pick what to double down on. Adam’s words, rule 4: “Favor data not rules — Let patterns in actual usage drive decisions”.

## The habit, using Adam’s Habit form

Adam’s four directives are quoted. The mapping under each one is **My dot**.

- “Make it Obvious”: The cue is a shining example catching Adam’s attention. Ideas already hit him on runs, and he records them: “Common scenario is that I am out for a run and have an idea hit me.” He answers only the first question.

- “Make it Easy”: One box is enough for one day. The first answers pile up, and that pile is the volume.

- “Make it Attractive”: Adam records when the third question clicks, which is when unrelated examples connect. That moment is the pull.

- “Make it Satisfying”: The video counts when it is published. Adam: “until another person has the opportunity to press "pay" "start trial" or "subscribe" (email newsletter, social media account) the product has not been built.”
